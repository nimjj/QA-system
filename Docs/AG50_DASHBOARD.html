AG50: how the three engines did on 50 independent long calls

50 support calls of 10 to 20 minutes, written and graded by Antigravity (a different AI from the one that wrote our training data). Nothing here is human-labelled. Every interval is a 95% bootstrap interval that resamples whole calls.

1. The headline: how often does each engine agree with the key?

Only the 7 model-judged items are a fair test of the models. The other 4 items are fixed rules that the app runs the same way whichever model is plugged in.

2. Is the gap real or just luck?

Paired comparison on the same 350 judged items. "Only A right" counts items where one engine was right and the other wrong. McNemar's exact test asks whether that split could be chance.

3. Item by item

Balanced accuracy averages "catches real faults" and "leaves good calls alone". 0.50 is a coin flip, 1.00 is perfect. Whiskers are 95% intervals; with 10 to 19 faults per item they are wide.

4. What kind of mistakes?

Missed fault: the key says FAIL and the engine said PASS. False alarm: the key says PASS and the engine said FAIL. Bars show counts out of 50 calls.

5. Where do the faults get lost? (fine-tuned Laya)

Laya never sees a whole call. The app cuts out small snippets first. For each keyed fault, was it even shown to the model?

6. Final scores

Each dot is one call. On the diagonal means the engine gave the same score as the key.
